Halal and Muslim-friendly dining across Japan.

Search restaurants by prefecture and city, from Tokyo, Osaka and Kyoto to Hokkaido, Fukuoka and Okinawa. Many Japanese restaurants offer a halal menu rather than a fully halal kitchen, so each listing shows the scope of the halal offer, alcohol and pork on the premises, and whether a reservation is needed, where the source states it.

How listings are classified

These labels can apply together. Each listing shows its sources and the date it was last checked.

Halal-certifiedCertification confirmed with the certifying body or its official register, with the date checked.
Certification reportedThe establishment, a tourism body or the press reports certification; not yet confirmed with the certifier.
Self-declaredThe establishment publicly states its own practices (for example a halal menu, no pork, no alcohol).
Selected halal menuOnly some dishes or a special menu are offered as halal; the rest of the menu may not be.
Muslim-ownedShown only where a credible source states it. It is not a halal certification.
Community-suggestedReported by the public (for example OpenStreetMap); not verified.
